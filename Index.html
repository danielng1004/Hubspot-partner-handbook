6Offset Your Investments Scenarios

How you capitalise on deals to generate revenue and offset your investment

Closing just two deals can effectively cover your initial investment. For example, if Customer A signs an annual contract for HubSpot Marketing Hub worth AUD $16,560 ($10,680 USD), a 20% revenue share generates $3,312 AUD ($2,136 USD) in year one from that deal alone. Try the revenue calculator ↗ to forecast your numbers.

You can also waive HubSpot's direct onboarding fees (typically AUD $4,320–$8,640) and bundle onboarding into your own service package. Partners commonly charge $6,000–$100,000+ for onboarding, implementation, consulting, and post-launch support. For complex CMS or integration projects, services can reach up to 5× the software cost.

A retainer model adds another strong revenue stream — generating recurring monthly retainers of several thousand dollars per client.
